# Kalam Claim Registry

**Status:** Active governance artifact

| Claim class | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by current product behavior or authoritative evidence | Kalam is an Enterprise Workforce Capability Platform |
| QUALIFIED | True only with explicit conditions | Integrations are scoped to supported connectors and implementation commitments |
| ASPIRATIONAL | Clearly identified as future intent | Future global expansion |
| PROHIBITED | Unsupported, misleading or unverifiable | Guaranteed ROI; independently certified compliance; autonomous competence certification |

## Required review

Before publishing a material claim about:

- security
- privacy
- compliance
- certification
- customers
- partnerships
- integrations
- AI capability
- performance
- ROI
- availability
- regulatory coverage
- geographic reach

the owner must identify evidence and approval.

## Canonical safe framing

Kalam supports accountable human decision-making with connected capability, evidence and readiness context. It does not independently certify competence, guarantee regulatory compliance or replace accountable management decisions.

## Release rule

No P0/P1 claim defect may ship. Unsupported claims must be removed, qualified or supported before publication.
